Research Paper AI Assistant

GenAI tool that reads paper abstracts and delivers structured summaries, contributions, and Q&A.

A GenAI-powered tool that reads academic paper abstracts or excerpts and instantly delivers structured summaries, extracted contributions, methodology identification, and follow-up Q&A — through a clean web interface.

What it does. Paste any research paper abstract or excerpt, and the assistant summarises it in plain English, extracts key contributions as a structured list, identifies the methodology, highlights limitations, and answers follow-up questions.

Why it matters. A practical LLM use case beyond text generation: reading, structuring, and reasoning about existing scientific content — built to support literature review and academic writing workflows.

Stack. Python, LLM APIs, web interface.

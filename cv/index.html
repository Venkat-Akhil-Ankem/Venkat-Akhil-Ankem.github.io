Professional Summary

Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering (Operations Research) at Polytechnique Montréal / GERAD, with a three-year MITACS-funded industrial collaboration inside Rio Tinto’s mine-planning operations (2022–2025) and continued monthly advisory since. Delivered cutting-plane and rolling-horizon methods achieving a 90% reduction in mine-scheduling computation time and an 80% reduction in time-to-feasibility, deployed into Rio Tinto production planning workflows. Published in Computers & Operations Research (2026); 5 peer-reviewed journal publications in total. Current doctoral work integrates machine learning into MILP solution strategies — instance-difficulty prediction and solver guidance on 100,000+ variable instances.

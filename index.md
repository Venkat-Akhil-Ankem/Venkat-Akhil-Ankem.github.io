## What I do

I build optimization models, algorithms, and research software in **C++** and **Python**, combining mixed-integer programming with cutting planes, relax-and-repair heuristics, rolling-horizon decomposition, and — in my current doctoral work — graph neural networks and supervised ML that predict which MILP instances will be hard to solve and guide the solver accordingly, trained on benchmark datasets built from Rio Tinto's real mine models (100,000+ variable instances).

My doctoral work applies this to the **Open-Pit Mine Production Scheduling Problem**: multi-period, precedence-constrained planning at a scale where exact methods alone don't finish in time and heuristics alone aren't strong enough. → [Full research focus](research)

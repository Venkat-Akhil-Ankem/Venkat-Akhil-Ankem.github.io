A faithful implementation of the Deep Q-Network (Mnih et al., 2015), written **from scratch in PyTorch/Gymnasium** as a deep-RL foundations exercise.

**Implementation.**
- Experience replay buffer and target network with periodic hard updates
- ε-greedy exploration with decay schedule
- Huber loss and gradient clipping for training stability
- Unit-tested with pytest

**Stack.** Python, PyTorch, Gymnasium, pytest.

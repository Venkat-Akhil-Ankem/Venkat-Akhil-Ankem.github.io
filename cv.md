## Summary

Ph.D. Candidate in Industrial Engineering (Operations Research) at Polytechnique Montréal and GERAD, with a three-year MITACS-funded industrial collaboration inside Rio Tinto's mine-planning operations (2022–2025) and continued monthly advisory ties since. Delivered cutting-plane and rolling-horizon methods achieving a 90% reduction in mine-scheduling computation time and an 80% reduction in time-to-feasibility, deployed directly into Rio Tinto production planning workflows. Author of 5 peer-reviewed journal publications, including first-author work in *Computers & Operations Research* (2026). Current doctoral work integrates machine learning into MILP solution strategies — instance-difficulty prediction and solver guidance on benchmark datasets built from 100,000+ variable mine-model instances.
